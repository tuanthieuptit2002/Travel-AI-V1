# Da Nang Local Food

Da Nang dining highlights include mì Quảng (turmeric noodles with pork or shrimp), bánh xèo, and fresh seafood along the Han River.

For a casual lunch, look for clay-pot rice (cơm niêu) and local vegetable sides. Evening seafood dinners are popular near the riverfront; order grilled fish, clams, and morning glory.

Budget travelers can eat well for under a few hundred thousand VND per person at local spots, while riverside restaurants are typically a medium price level.

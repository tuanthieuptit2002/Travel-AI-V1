# Customs, Safety, and Seasonal Tips

Dress modestly at temples and pagodas. Remove shoes when entering homes and many religious sites. A polite greeting and using both hands when giving money or gifts is appreciated.

Keep valuables secure in crowded markets and on overnight buses. Use licensed transport when possible, especially at night.

Vietnam's weather is regional: the south is tropical year-round, the center can see flooding in autumn, and the north has cooler winters. Pack light rain protection and check seasonal patterns for your destination before locking outdoor plans.

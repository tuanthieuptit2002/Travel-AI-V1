# Getting Around Vietnam

Domestic flights connect major hubs quickly. Trains are comfortable for scenic routes, while ride-hailing apps work well inside cities.

Between nearby Central Vietnam destinations such as Da Nang and Hoi An, private cars and shared vans are common. Motorbike rentals require a valid license and careful local driving awareness.

For airport transfers, arrange transport in advance during holidays. Keep small cash for taxis and short rides when digital payments are unavailable.

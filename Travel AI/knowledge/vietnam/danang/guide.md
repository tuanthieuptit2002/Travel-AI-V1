# Da Nang Destination Guide

Da Nang is a coastal gateway between Hue and Hoi An, known for My Khe Beach, the Marble Mountains, and the Dragon Bridge fire show.

Mornings are ideal for swimming or cycling the beachfront. Afternoons suit Son Tra Peninsula viewpoints and Linh Ung Pagoda. Evenings center on the Han River and Bach Dang street food.

Travelers who like photography should plan around sunrise at the beach and weekend bridge shows. The city is more modern and spacious than Hoi An, making it a comfortable base for Central Vietnam itineraries.

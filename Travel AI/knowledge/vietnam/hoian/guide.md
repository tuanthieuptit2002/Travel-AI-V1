# Hoi An Ancient Town Tips

Hoi An's lantern-lit old town is a UNESCO site best explored on foot in the late afternoon and evening. Tailors, cafes, and the Japanese Covered Bridge are signature stops.

Visit earlier in the day to avoid peak crowds, then return after sunset for photography. Renting a bicycle is useful for An Bang Beach and nearby villages.

Bring cash for market snacks and small shops. Many historic streets are pedestrian-priority in the evening.

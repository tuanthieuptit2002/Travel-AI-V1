# Hanoi Old Quarter Essentials

Hanoi blends lakeside walking routes with dense Old Quarter streets. Hoan Kiem Lake and the Temple of Literature are classic first-day anchors.

Traffic is intense; use marked crossings and move deliberately with groups when crossing larger roads. Egg coffee, bun cha, and pho are reliable local food starting points.

Autumn and early winter are popular for milder temperatures, while summer can be hot and humid with afternoon storms.

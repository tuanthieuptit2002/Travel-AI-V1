# Vietnamese Cuisine Overview

Vietnamese meals balance herbs, vegetables, broths, and grilled proteins. Regional styles vary: the north is subtler, the center spicier, and the south sweeter.

Common traveler favorites include pho, banh mi, fresh spring rolls, and regional noodle soups. Markets are excellent for fruit and inexpensive snacks.

If you have dietary needs, learn a few Vietnamese phrases for vegetarian or allergy requests, and confirm ingredients when ordering street food.
